# Relational Buildblog

Status: format protocol / relation-loyal / not raw-publication

This document defines how the live collaboration between THE ARCHITECT and THE TRANSLATOR can become a publishable format without becoming a betrayal of the relation.

---

## Core Law

> The relation is not the content. The relation is the engine.

The public form may be driven by the collision between two operator modes, but it must never publish the private relation as raw material.

---

## What the Format Is

A relational buildblog is a processed, pseudonymized, structurally translated public account of a shared attempt to build future-facing products under AI-era conditions.

It can include:

- build logs
- idea collisions
- reconstructed dialogues
- public lab notes
- weekly proof surfaces
- failed category attempts
- app concepts that mutate into content formats
- content formats that mutate back into app requirements
- reflections on what the build activated

It should read like a live product studio discovering what it is while building.

---

## What the Format Is Not

It is not:

- a dump of private messages
- a family-conflict product
- a consultant case study
- a superiority performance by THE TRANSLATOR
- a comedy where THE ARCHITECT becomes the punchline
- a diagnosis of THE ARCHITECT
- a public correction of THE ARCHITECT's business instincts

---

## Comedy Rule

> No one is the idiot alone. The idiocy belongs to the machine.

The humor must arise from the shared machine:

- too many possible products
- unclear relation between app and income
- existing platforms used as laboratories for challengers
- origin sovereignty colliding with practical reuse
- AI accelerating surface before proof catches up
- public formats emerging from private confusion

THE ARCHITECT is never the joke. THE TRANSLATOR is never the judge. The machine is the unstable comic object.

---

## Sovereignty Rule

THE ARCHITECT must remain recognizable as builder, not specimen.

THE TRANSLATOR must remain recognizable as translator, not owner of the truth.

The public form must preserve both capacities.

---

## Processing Rule

Raw interaction cannot enter the format directly.

Every public piece must pass through:

1. **Pseudonymization** — no real names, domains, company names, or identifying markers.
2. **Structural translation** — what kind of mechanism appeared?
3. **Control-room reading** — what new mastery domain did the situation activate?
4. **Relation check** — does the piece preserve dignity on both sides?
5. **Public value check** — does the piece teach, amuse, clarify, or produce transferable insight beyond the private relation?

---

## Episode Unit

A relational buildblog episode should be built around one collision.

Examples:

| Collision | Hidden control room |
|---|---|
| A platform challenger is proposed before the value surface is tested | Revenue / Reality |
| A prior build returns but cannot own the origin | Survival / Legibility |
| A high-difficulty money-machine appears as challenge-object | Revenue / Accountability |
| A niche content product becomes app idea, then publishing system | Behavior / Legibility |
| A public lab becomes more valuable than the initial product idea | Reality / Legibility |

---

## Possible Public Forms

- weekly build diary
- short annotated lab notes
- pseudonymized "machine episode" posts
- public postmortems without failure language
- paid supporter notes
- small essays about AI-era product formation
- comedic field reports from the zero-point

---

## Ethical Boundary

The relational buildblog is allowed only if it strengthens the relation or protects it while making a public artifact.

If a piece requires humiliating THE ARCHITECT, exposing private material, or presenting THE TRANSLATOR as the adult in the room, the piece is invalid.
