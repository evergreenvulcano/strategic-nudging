# Appendix A — Product Proof Checklist

> *Not a list of what is missing. A list of what, when present, makes the empire undeniable.*

This checklist is structured as a **next proof level** — the evidence that would allow each sector to become legible to partners, employers, investors, or customers. Complete it for the flagship sector first.

---

## Section 1: Existence Proof

These items confirm the product exists and functions as described.

- [ ] The platform loads correctly on desktop and mobile
- [ ] Core user flows complete without errors (sign up, post, search, contact)
- [ ] Premium/paid tier is purchasable and delivers its promised value
- [ ] AI-assisted features function as described in product copy
- [ ] All external integrations (payment, partner feeds, APIs) are live and stable

---

## Section 2: User Proof

These items confirm that real users are engaging with the product.

- [ ] Monthly active user count is defined and tracked
- [ ] User registration is open and functioning
- [ ] At least one user cohort (30+ days old) shows return engagement
- [ ] The top 10 user actions on the platform are known and measured
- [ ] Users can articulate the product's value in their own words (qualitative evidence)

---

## Section 3: Content/Supply Proof

For a marketplace: supply is half the product. These items confirm the marketplace has meaningful supply.

- [ ] Active listing count is tracked
- [ ] Content freshness is measured (listings created in last 30 days)
- [ ] Minimum viable supply density is defined for the flagship sector
- [ ] Supply quality is assessed (are listings complete? are they real?)

---

## Section 4: Commercial Proof

These items confirm the empire has economic life.

- [ ] At least one paying user exists
- [ ] Monthly recurring revenue (MRR) is defined and tracked
- [ ] Conversion rate from free to paid is known
- [ ] The primary monetization vector is explicitly documented
- [ ] Platform operating costs (hosting, tools, services) are known and covered

---

## Section 5: Search Presence Proof

These items confirm the empire is findable without paid acquisition.

- [ ] Primary target keywords are identified for the flagship sector
- [ ] Search ranking for target keywords is tracked
- [ ] Organic traffic volume is known and measured monthly
- [ ] At least one organic traffic source (search, direct, referral) is growing

---

## Section 6: Stability Proof

These items confirm the empire can sustain operation without the operator's continuous intervention.

- [ ] The platform runs without manual intervention for at least 7 consecutive days
- [ ] Errors and downtime events are logged and reviewed
- [ ] A basic incident response posture exists (what to do when something breaks)
- [ ] Platform costs are known and not growing unexpectedly

---

*Complete this checklist per sector. SECTOR PRIME first.*
