# Appendix B — Launch & Operations Checklist

> *The difference between a product that exists and a product that operates is operational intentionality.*

This checklist covers the operational layer — the systems and postures that allow the empire to function reliably at scale without requiring constant manual intervention.

---

## Section 1: Launch Readiness

- [ ] The platform has been tested by at least 3 people who are not THE ARCHITECT
- [ ] Critical user flows have been tested on mobile
- [ ] Error states (failed payment, empty search results, missing content) are handled gracefully
- [ ] Contact or support mechanism exists for users who need help
- [ ] Platform availability monitoring is in place (uptime alerts)

---

## Section 2: User Onboarding

- [ ] New user onboarding communicates the platform's value within the first session
- [ ] Registration flow is optimized to minimize drop-off
- [ ] First action after signup is clear and achievable
- [ ] Welcome communication (email or in-app) exists and is intentional

---

## Section 3: Content Operations

- [ ] Process exists for removing invalid, spam, or abusive listings
- [ ] Minimum content quality standards are defined (even informally)
- [ ] Process for handling duplicate or stale listings exists
- [ ] Featured or premium content is distinguishable from standard content

---

## Section 4: Support Operations

- [ ] A support contact channel exists and is monitored
- [ ] Response time expectation is set (even if just a policy: "we respond within X days")
- [ ] Common user questions are identified and answered in an FAQ or help section
- [ ] Escalation path for serious issues (fraud, abuse, legal) is defined

---

## Section 5: Business Continuity

- [ ] Platform can operate for 30 days without new development work
- [ ] Dependencies (third-party services, APIs, hosting) are documented
- [ ] Payment provider relationship is stable and understood
- [ ] Backup or recovery process exists for critical platform data

---

## Section 6: Partner Operations (if applicable)

- [ ] Data ingestion from partner feeds is automated or has a defined cadence
- [ ] Partner relationship is documented (agreement, contact, SLA expectations)
- [ ] Partner feed failures are detected and handled without user-facing impact
- [ ] Revenue or value-sharing arrangement is formalized

---

*Review this checklist at each significant growth milestone.*
